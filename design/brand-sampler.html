Graphite Meter · brand sampler

01 Carbon

Graphite as engineered material: carbon lattice, conductivity, metallic luster. The interface is nearly monochrome — cool, neutral greys with a clear metallic step between surfaces — and a single conductive teal carries everything live. Cold steel blue marks reference and at-rest states. The data provides the rest of the color.

02 Grade

The drawing pencil as a designed object: deep lacquer green, a brass ferrule, a graphite core. The 9H–9B hardness scale is the original graphite meter — grade-stamp lettering becomes the label motif (note the boxed eyebrows). Greys stay strictly neutral; all warmth lives in the brass.

03 Observatory

Night-shift instrumentation — an observatory control room. Deep blue-ink darks stepped in distinct layers, ice-blue and aurora-green readouts, amber reserved for caution. The most atmospheric of the three; light mode is the same room by day.
